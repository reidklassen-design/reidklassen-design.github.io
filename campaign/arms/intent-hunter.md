# Arm A — Intent Hunter

## Objective

Respond to people already asking for a small app, firmware change, integration,
or focused automation. This arm wins through relevance and speed, not volume.

## Source priority

1. Contra opportunities and inbound requests.
2. Upwork projects whose credible budget supports a quote of at least CAD $120.
3. LinkedIn Service Page requests.
4. Hiring/community threads only when their rules explicitly allow independent
   project replies. Hacker News text must be written personally by Reid.

Do not scrape or automate a personal-account search. The campaign can maintain
public source URLs; Reid opens and evaluates protected-platform results.

## Qualification score

Score each item from 0–10. It is qualified at 8 or more with no hard reject.

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit test |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Outcome clarity | 0–2 | Names a concrete result, not “build me an app” |
| Starter fit | 0–2 | Fits a starter scope or has budget for a fixed quote |
| Budget evidence | 0–2 | Stated budget or credible paid-project history supports the quote |
| Delivery fit | 0–1 | Can be proven in a short, bounded build |
| Buyer credibility | 0–1 | Specific context and a consistent platform presence |
| Dependency/safety | 0–1 | No prohibited work and no unknown dependency blocking a quote |
| Acceptance test | 0–1 | Success can be stated as an observable behaviour |

Hard rejects: unpaid sample work, vague revenue share, request to move an
Upwork/Fiverr conversation off-platform, suspicious files, credential requests,
unsafe or prohibited work, impossible deadline, or a budget below platform
floor.

## Workflow

1. Open the original request manually and record only its public URL and a
   private lead identifier.
2. Score it before drafting. Record one sentence of evidence for every point.
3. Reject below 8/10. Do not “round up” to meet the sample target.
4. Draft a two-behaviour smallest-useful version and one acceptance test.
5. Choose one truthful portfolio proof that matches the work.
6. Quote a starting point or fixed price that respects channel fees and scope.
7. Add the source code and run the policy preflight.
8. Reid reopens the source, verifies that it is still active, rewrites the first
   line in his voice, and submits manually.
9. Log the final copy, Connect/fee cost, time, and outcome privately.
10. If the client replies, produce a build card before accepting payment.

## Proposal structure

Use [the intent proposal](../templates/messages.md#intent-hunter-proposal). It
must contain:

- One line proving the request was read.
- The two behaviours in the smallest useful version.
- One platform/device assumption.
- One price statement with a boundary.
- One relevant proof link if the platform permits it.
- One easy question that advances scope.

No biography paragraph, generic enthusiasm, invented deadline, or pressure.

## Minimum sample and review

- 12 qualified, submitted proposals per generation.
- Inspect after proposal 6 for obvious policy or targeting failure, but do not
  eliminate before 12 unless there are two paid bookings or a safety stop.
- Record proposal-to-reply, reply-to-qualified-brief, qualified-brief-to-quote,
  and quote-to-payment rates.
