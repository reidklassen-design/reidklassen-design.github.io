# Arm C — Partner Relay

## Objective

Become the reliable small-project option for professionals whose clients ask
for work outside their core service. The partner keeps the client relationship;
Reid supplies a bounded build.

## Partner fit

Prioritize web designers, repair shops, electronics specialists, makerspaces,
3D-printing services, bookkeepers, and marketing freelancers who:

- Serve real small businesses or makers.
- Publicly describe a complementary service.
- Do not already advertise the same custom-development offer.
- Have a lawful, role-relevant contact route or accept platform messages.
- Can plausibly encounter a tiny app, workflow, or device request.

Reject mass directories, harvested lists, inactive businesses, personal contact
details, direct competitors, or partners in prohibited/safety-critical work.

## Workflow

1. Identify a public business page or a warm referral.
2. Write one sentence describing the exact gap Reid can cover for that partner.
3. Choose a permitted contact route. Prefer a warm introduction, platform-native
   message, or published business inquiry form over cold email.
4. For direct email, document the consent basis and insert the required CASL
   identity, mailing address, contact method, and unsubscribe text. If any field
   is missing, do not send.
5. Use the partner introduction template, personalized to one observable service.
6. Reid verifies the business and sends manually.
7. If the partner replies “menu,” send only the three bounded starter scopes.
8. Agree in writing that the partner owns their client relationship, neither
   party may make claims for the other, and client details are shared only with
   client permission.
9. Log the outcome. One lawful follow-up maximum after seven days; none after an
   opt-out, rejection, or complaint.

## Referral model

Do not offer cash commission at founding prices. Offer reciprocal referrals and,
after a successful paid delivery, one additional refinement capped at 30 minutes
for a successful referrer. Disclose the referral relationship to the client.

## Minimum sample and success

- Ten verified, individually relevant approaches per generation.
- Count an approach only after Reid sends it through a permitted route.
- Track replies, “menu” requests, introduced qualified briefs, paid bookings,
  contribution margin, opt-outs, complaints, and review time.
