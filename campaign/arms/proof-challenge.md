# Arm B — Proof Challenge

## Objective

Earn inbound briefs by making small custom software feel understandable and
attainable. Every post teaches something useful even if the reader never buys.

## Channel order

1. LinkedIn personal profile and Service Page.
2. Nextdoor Business Page for a genuinely local offer.
3. One correctly categorized Craigslist services post.
4. Relevant communities whose written rules allow helpful replies or offers.
5. Fiverr gig discovery, with all interaction remaining on Fiverr.

Hacker News is not a prepared-content channel. Reid may participate personally
under its guidelines, but those actions do not count toward this arm's prepared
post quota.

## Content pillars

- **Boundary proof:** show exactly what a CAD $40 or CAD $60 starter can and
  cannot include.
- **One-annoyance prompt:** ask for a repeated task and offer a short smallest-
  useful-tool outline.
- **Build proof:** show one real interface and explain the before/after in plain
  language.
- **Decision aid:** explain when a tiny custom tool is a better fit than a
  subscription or spreadsheet.

Use only authentic project images and outcomes. Never imply that a concept,
coming-soon project, or generated visual is completed client work.

## Workflow

1. Select one audience, one pain, and one proof asset.
2. Check the community's current rules and capture the rule URL privately.
3. Draft a 70–160 word post or a 40–100 word helpful reply.
4. Give the useful answer first. Include an offer only where promotion is
   explicitly permitted or the person asked for a provider.
5. Add one CTA and one source-coded link where links are allowed.
6. Apply the truth, privacy, scope, and channel gates.
7. Reid previews the exact placement and posts manually.
8. Reply to real questions with specifics; do not manufacture engagement or ask
   friends to boost the post.
9. Log the action and any qualified brief privately.

## Minimum sample and cadence

- Four original proof posts and twelve substantive replies per 14 days.
- Space original posts across the generation; never repeat identical copy.
- A substantive reply solves part of the stated problem without requiring a
  click. Emoji-only, praise-only, and generic sales replies do not count.

## Success measures

Track qualified briefs per action, source-coded inquiries, quote value,
payments, moderation events, and Reid time. Impressions, reactions, and follows
are diagnostic only and never decide the surviving arm.
