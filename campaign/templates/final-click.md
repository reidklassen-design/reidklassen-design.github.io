# Ten-Minute Final-Click Checklist

Run once daily. The queue may prepare research and drafts; Reid makes every
external decision and click.

## Global stop check

- [ ] Fewer than four active builds.
- [ ] Fewer than five paid founding projects.
- [ ] No unresolved policy warning, complaint, refund, or opt-out affects this
      person, community, channel, or variant.
- [ ] Spend after this batch remains within the unlocked cash ceiling.

## For each action

- [ ] Open the original source and confirm it is current.
- [ ] Confirm the recipient/community asked for or permits this interaction.
- [ ] Confirm the lead score, source code, and public URL match the draft.
- [ ] Replace every placeholder and remove unsupported claims.
- [ ] Verify the smallest useful version and acceptance test are technically
      credible.
- [ ] Verify currency, starting-price boundary, exclusions, and platform fee.
- [ ] Verify the portfolio proof is real and relevant.
- [ ] Verify the message contains no private client information.
- [ ] If direct email, verify recorded consent basis, identity, mailing address,
      valid contact method, and unsubscribe instruction.
- [ ] Rewrite the opening in Reid's voice.
- [ ] Preview the exact destination, audience, and attachments.
- [ ] Click once manually. Stop if the platform warns, blocks, or changes terms.
- [ ] Record timestamp, final copy, action cost, review minutes, and outcome in
      the private ledger.

## Inbox priority

1. Answer active client and partner replies.
2. Handle explicit opt-outs immediately and update suppression.
3. Submit near-deadline qualified proposals.
4. Publish approved helpful replies and proof posts.
5. Send approved partner introductions.

## End of batch

- [ ] No action was left in an ambiguous “maybe sent” state.
- [ ] Paid projects and active-build counts are current.
- [ ] Tomorrow's queue excludes every suppressed contact.
- [ ] Any warning is attached to the variant and pauses it pending review.
