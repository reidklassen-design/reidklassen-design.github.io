# Offer Menu

## Short profile

I build focused phone apps, desktop tools, and MCU firmware for people who need
one useful job done without turning it into a giant software project. Show me
the annoying task, the device it should run on, and what success looks like. I'll
recommend the smallest useful version and quote it before work begins.

## Direct starter menu

### Phone app — starting at CAD $60

One iOS or Android app with one focused flow of about one to three screens,
local data, source, a test build, and one revision.

Not included at the starter price: accounts, payments, remote services, store
submission, third-party charges, a second platform, or continuing support.

### Desktop app — starting at CAD $60

One app for one target desktop platform, with one window and one local
input-to-output workflow. Includes source, a packaged build, and one revision.

Additional screens, accounts, remote services, installers for multiple
platforms, and continuing support receive a fixed quote.

### MCU firmware — starting at CAD $40

One supported development board, one low-voltage peripheral, and one defined
behaviour. Includes source, pinout, flash notes, and one revision. The client
supplies reproducible hardware.

Not available for medical, automotive, mains/high-voltage, access-control,
weapons, emergency, or other safety-critical uses.

## What to send

1. What should it do?
2. Where should it run?
3. What would make it useful?

The first five paid projects receive founding-client pricing. A spot is reserved
only after scope, price, and payment are confirmed.
